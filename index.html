Frequently asked questions

Find answers about using the NexaTech demonstration.

What can I explore in NexaTech?

Open the dashboard to review customer records, orders, performance trends, and report downloads.

How do the plans differ?

Starter covers the essentials, Professional adds analytics and reports, and Enterprise illustrates a multi-workspace offering.

How can I open the dashboard?

Use the Dashboard link in the navigation. The sample workspace is open to visitors.

Can I filter customers?

Yes. The Customers page supports search by name, email, or company and a status filter.

What happens when I register?

The form checks the sample inputs locally and displays demo feedback.

What kind of reports are available?

You can download sample sales, revenue, customer, or product performance data as a CSV.
